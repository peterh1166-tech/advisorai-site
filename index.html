AdvisorAI

AdvisorAI is a private personal productivity application used by its owner to support educational content-development and review workflows.

What AdvisorAI does

AdvisorAI helps generate, review, organize, and deliver draft educational content for human review. It may use a dedicated Gmail account to send review packages and to read feedback replies sent back to that dedicated account.

AdvisorAI is not a public service and is not offered for use by clients or the general public. Human review and any required compliance approval remain separate from the application.

Google data use

Google account access is used only to support the application's email workflow, including sending review emails and reading relevant feedback replies in the dedicated AdvisorAI Gmail account.

For more information, see the AdvisorAI Privacy Policy.
